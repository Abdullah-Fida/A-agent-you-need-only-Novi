Stop publishing the model's thinking; guarantee an image; six posts a day

A week of live output showed 27 of 191 published posts were the model
narrating instead of answering — "We need to adapt the given text into a
Facebook caption...", "Here's a thinking process:". The signal group was
worst hit at 19 of 80. All of it went out verbatim.

Cause: every task routes through openrouter/free, which regularly serves a
reasoning model, and the only guard was two phrases checked in the first 200
characters. Nothing else inspected the output before publishing.

- AIEngine now strips visible chain-of-thought (<think> tags closed or cut
  off, "Final answer:" labels, whole-reply code fences) and rejects a reply
  that is still narration, retrying on a different model rather than the same
  one. Verified against all 191 real posts: 27 of 27 leaks caught, zero false
  positives on the 164 good ones.
- generate() takes an optional validator so a caller can require output that
  is actually publishable.
- Signal copier supplies one: a real coin pair, trade fields and the footer,
  or a bare REJECT. Narrated rejections no longer count. If nothing valid
  survives the retries the signal is dropped, because posting unvalidated
  output is what put the model's deliberation in the group.
- Facebook captions fall back to the Telegram copy when a rewrite is doubtful.

Images
- A post whose picture is only the fallback card is deferred 30 minutes and
  retried with fresh stories, up to three attempts, then published anyway so
  the slot is never silently lost. Failed and errored posts requeue the same
  way; previously a slot was marked fired before the attempt, so any failure
  cost a post for the day.

Six posts a day
- Two of the six slots could never fire: 00:40 sat inside the 23:00-07:00
  sleep window, and 13:40 was a leftover makeup slot 40 minutes after 13:00.
  Replaced with six evenly spread waking-hour slots.

Retired models
- Groq removed the entire Llama family from this account. NOVI's chat model
  llama-3.1-8b-instant now 404s, which is why NOVI stopped answering, and
  ARTICLE_MODEL llama-3.3-70b-versatile is equally dead. Confirmed against
  the live model list. NOVI moves to openai/gpt-oss-20b; both were tested for
  narration before selection. The engine now switches models on a
  model-not-found error instead of retrying a model that cannot recover.

Story selection
- Major world events (war, elections, central banks, disasters) score above
  routine coverage, and roundups and filler are penalised. "Here's what
  happened in crypto today" — the headline behind the vaguest article on the
  site — now scores below zero and will not be selected.

ARTICLE_MODEL must be changed to openai/gpt-oss-120b in Render; the value in
render.env is updated but Render holds its own copy.

267 tests pass, 39 of them new and drawn from the real failures.

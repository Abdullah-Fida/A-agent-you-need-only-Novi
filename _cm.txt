Heal from retired models automatically; move the News AI to Groq

Answering "why does this break every day": the cause is not a bad key. Two
providers were failing in different ways at the same time.

OpenRouter's free tier is exhausted — every call returns 429 with "Rate limit
exceeded: free-models-per-day. Add 10 credits to unlock 1000 free model
requests per day". The News AI ran on it, and the News AI produces every post,
signal, caption and stealth reply.

Groq is fine: the key authenticates and offers 1000 requests a day. The bot
needs about 53. What broke there was the model names — Groq retired the whole
Llama family, so llama-3.1-8b-instant (NOVI) and llama-3.3-70b-versatile
(articles) both 404.

- AIEngine reads the provider's live model catalogue and substitutes a working
  model when the configured one is gone, preferring a known-good list and
  falling back to any chat model. Confirmed against the live API: asking for
  llama-3.3-70b-versatile now resolves to openai/gpt-oss-120b and generates
  normally. A retirement is reported once by email as informational, since
  nothing is broken and nothing needs doing immediately.
- The News AI moves to Groq, which has the headroom.
- NEWS_API_PROVIDER and the key list were independent, so pointing the News AI
  at Groq would have kept sending an OpenRouter key and failed authentication.
  The provider now selects its own keys.

Signals retry harder before being dropped: six attempts across different
models rather than three. They stay immediate rather than deferred — a signal
re-sent half an hour later quotes prices that have moved, which is worse than
not sending it.

Render still holds its own copy of these values and must be updated:
  NEWS_API_PROVIDER=groq
  NEWS_MODEL=openai/gpt-oss-120b
  ARTICLE_MODEL=openai/gpt-oss-120b

267 tests pass.

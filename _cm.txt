Route short tasks to the small model instead of pinning one model

Setting NEWS_MODEL bypassed per-task routing entirely, so every task on the
News AI — including one-line trading signals, Facebook captions and SEO
metadata — ran on gpt-oss-120b. Groq allows 8000 tokens per minute per key,
so spending the large model on a six-line signal takes headroom the news post
and the article need, and signals are the one output where latency matters.

Tasks now carry a tier. Long-form work (the channel post, the article) keeps
the quality model; short structured work (signal cleansing, captions, SEO,
stealth replies) uses the provider's small model. A configured NEWS_MODEL or
ARTICLE_MODEL still sets the quality tier, so explicit configuration is
honoured without pinning everything to it.

Checked against the live API with the real signal prompt before switching:
both models keep a valid signal 3 out of 3 times, and the small model still
rejects locked teasers, adverts and partial signals correctly, with the
footer intact and the competitor's handle removed. An earlier false rejection
came from a shortened test prompt, not from the model.

News and article agents remain on separate Groq keys, so their rate limits
stay independent.

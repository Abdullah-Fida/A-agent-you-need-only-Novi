Complete the Pinterest agent: copy, imaging, publishing, review, controls

Publishes through Buffer rather than Pinterest's own API. Pinterest grants
Trial access first, where every pin created is a sandbox entity visible only
to its creator, and Standard access needs a demo video and one to four weeks
of review. Buffer is an official Pinterest Marketing Partner, so this route
publishes real pins today and stays inside Pinterest's terms — which browser
automation would not. Confirmed against the live Buffer schema: pinterest is
a supported service and a pin's board, title and destination link go in
metadata.pinterest.

Uses a second Buffer account, separate from the one Novi posts Facebook with,
so they do not share the free plan's three channels and ten-post queue. A full
queue is treated as a full queue, not an error, and retried next cycle.

- content: pin copy through Novi's AIEngine, so it inherits the narration
  guards; a model answering "We need to write a Pinterest title..." would
  otherwise be published, which is what happened on the news side. Six angles
  rotate so the same product does not always read the same way. The #ad
  disclosure is appended in code, never left to the model, because forgetting
  it once costs the account.
- imaging: 1000x1500 branded pin. AliExpress photos are square and often
  carry the seller's own text, so they are composited into a frame rather
  than posted as-is — that fixes the aspect ratio Pinterest ranks on and puts
  our wordmark on the image instead of another shop's. Renders with no photo
  and with no installed fonts.
- store: own tables in Novi's Supabase. Every call goes through
  asyncio.to_thread, because supabase-py is synchronous and awaiting it
  directly discards the coroutine silently.
- Category performance is learned over a 30-day window, not 24 hours:
  Pinterest is a search engine rather than a feed, so pins accumulate saves
  over weeks and a daily read would be fitting noise. Multipliers are clamped
  so one lucky pin nudges the ranking rather than rewriting it.

Review is on by default and every pin waits for a decision. This recommends
purchases rather than opinions, so an invented feature is worth catching
before it publishes. Rejecting a pin also suppresses that product.

Controls: a dashboard toggle, four endpoints, and two NOVI commands. Off by
default and persisted across restarts, since it spends an affiliate quota and
posts to a public account.

upload_image now takes a bucket so pin images stay out of the article heroes.

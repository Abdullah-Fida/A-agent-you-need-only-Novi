===============================================================================
 TIDY NOOK — 28 DAYS OF PIN PROMPTS
 6 boards x 6 pins a day x 28 days = 168 prompts
 Four weeks, four completely different looks.
===============================================================================

HOW TO USE THIS FILE

Each week has ONE STYLE BLOCK. Paste that block, then the day's SCENE line,
then the FIXED TAIL at the bottom of this page. Three pieces, one prompt.

    [ WEEK STYLE BLOCK ]  +  [ SCENE ]  +  [ FIXED TAIL ]

The style block is what changes every Monday. The scene is different for all
168. The tail never changes.

WHY THE LOOK TURNS OVER WEEKLY, NOT DAILY
Six pins a day in one style read as a considered set. Six pins a day in six
styles read as six different accounts. The week is the unit a viewer feels
as "this is what this account looks like right now" — and it changes before
anyone tires of it.

THE SIX BOARDS, IN ORDER, EVERY DAY
    1. Bathroom Storage Ideas
    2. Pantry and Fridge Storage
    3. Under Sink and Cabinet Storage
    4. Tiny Apartment Solutions
    5. Kitchen Gadgets Worth Buying
    6. Small Kitchen Organization

TITLES
Every title below is 40–60 characters. That window gets roughly two thirds
more impressions than longer ones. No exclamation marks, no dates, no prices.

The title does NOT appear on the picture. The pin is the photograph, full
bleed, with nothing drawn over it — Pinterest shows the title and the
description beside the pin in its own type, so a caption burned into the
image is a second headline arguing with the first.

WORDS IN THE PICTURE (rare, and optional)
Almost no pin needs any. When one does, add an Overlay line to that entry:

    3.4  TINY APARTMENT SOLUTIONS
         Title:   Keep only this week's shoes by the front door
         Overlay: This week only
         Scene:   ...

Two or three words, no more — longer is a caption again. The model paints
them INTO the scene, chalked on a small board or printed on a label, so they
belong to the room rather than sitting on top of the photograph. Leave the
line out and the picture has no text at all, which is the default.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 FIXED TAIL — append to EVERY prompt, all 168
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Editorial interior photography for a premium home magazine. Photorealistic,
sharp, high detail, professionally styled and immaculately clean. Shot on a
full-frame camera with a 35mm lens at f/2.8, natural light only, true-to-life
colour, no colour cast.

Vertical portrait orientation, 2:3. Compose the subject in the CENTRE of the
frame and fill it — the very top and bottom edges will be cropped away, so
nothing important belongs there.

No text, no words, no letters, no numbers, no labels with writing, no logos,
no watermarks, no signage anywhere in the image. No people, no hands, no
faces. Not an illustration, not a 3D render, not a collage.

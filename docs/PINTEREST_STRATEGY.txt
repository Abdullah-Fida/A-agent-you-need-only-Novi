1. THE CATEGORY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Home and kitchen ORGANIZATION. Storage, not decor.

Wider than kitchen alone -- it covers bathroom, closet and small-apartment
storage, which is where the AliExpress inventory depth is -- but it deliberately
stops short of home decor.

Why storage beats decor, even though decor has more Pinterest traffic:

                        ORGANIZATION              DECOR
  What they want        a fix for a problem       inspiration, a mood
  What they do next     buy a $15 solution now    save it for "someday"
  Competing against     other product pins        professional photography
  AliExpress fit        good -- generic goods     poor -- taste-driven, ships
                                                  badly, high return rate

Somebody searching "under sink organizer" has a cupboard annoying them today.
Somebody searching "kitchen decor ideas" is daydreaming. The AliExpress
affiliate cookie is short, so slow-converting traffic is close to worthless.
That single fact is the whole argument.

REJECTED, and why:
  Jewelry / fashion    AliExpress listings are frequently counterfeit, and
                       counterfeit links are how affiliate accounts get
                       permanently banned. Not worth it at any commission.
  Electronics / phone  3-5% commission vs 7-9% here. Male-skewed, and weak on
                       Pinterest.
  Craft supplies       Pinterest-native, but order values too small to earn.
  Home decor           see the table above.


2. THE ECONOMICS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
AliExpress pays roughly 7-9% in Home & Kitchen.

PIN_MIN_PRICE is 12, raised from 3. At 8%, a $4 product earns about 30 cents
and costs exactly as much to build a pin for as a $40 one. Cheap listings also
have the worst photos and the longest shipping.
PIN_MAX_PRICE is 80.

HONEST CAVEAT, worth re-reading when results look slow: the AliExpress cookie
window is short and Pinterest traffic is slow by nature -- people save a pin now
and buy weeks later, often after it has expired. Judge this over months, not
days. It does not change the category choice; it sets the expectation.

4. THE BOARDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Six boards, all public. Pinterest distributes pins BY BOARD TOPIC, so the board
is the strongest signal for who gets shown the pin. One board holding everything
is topically incoherent and Pinterest cannot place it. Six tight boards are six
separate search surfaces. Six also stops 6-8 affiliate pins a day from looking
like dumping.

The serviceId is the id Pinterest itself uses, and is what Buffer's
boardServiceId field wants. Buffer's internal board id is a DIFFERENT number and
is rejected with "Board not found". These ids change if the channel is
disconnected and reconnected.

5. HOW PRODUCTS ARE FOUND
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
sourcing.py rotates 16 search terms, one per run. A category id alone returns
the same best-sellers every time and the dedupe gate starves within days.

Every term is PROBLEM-SHAPED, not browse-shaped. That is the category thesis
expressed in code:

  kitchen drawer organizer     pot lid organizer rack
  under sink organizer         stackable storage bin
  spice rack organizer         corner shelf organizer
  pantry storage container     bathroom storage shelf
  fridge organizer bin         wall mounted kitchen rack
  cabinet shelf riser          closet organizer box
  over the sink rack           kitchen sink caddy
  airtight food container      utensil holder organizer

Filters: min rating 4.3, min orders 100, price 12-80.


6. THE RULES THAT PROTECT THE ACCOUNT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
compliance.py is PURE CODE. No language model touches it. A model would be right
most of the time, and the failure mode is losing the account.

It refuses:
  - shortened or cloaked links (bit.ly, linktr.ee, ...) -- Pinterest reads these
    as spam
  - any host that is not AliExpress, including lookalikes such as
    aliexpress.com.attacker.net
  - a missing #ad disclosure
  - ANY price in the copy. AliExpress prices move constantly and a pin outlives
    them by months, so a stated price becomes a false claim.
  - duplicates, checked on product id, destination link AND image fingerprint,
    because AliExpress relists identical products under new ids


7. CADENCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PIN_MAX_PER_DAY=4 for the first ten days. A brand-new account posting eight
affiliate links a day is the textbook spam signature. Then raise to 8.
Pinterest guidance tops out near 15 and the config clamps there.
PIN_MIN_GAP_MINUTES=45.

PIN_REQUIRE_REVIEW stays on until the output has earned trust. This recommends
purchases rather than opinions, so catching an invented feature before it
publishes is cheap insurance.
